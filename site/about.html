My Philosophy of Education

I believe teaching goes beyond explaining lessons; it is about understanding the learners behind them. I hope to become a teacher who uses differentiated learning to meet individual needs, gives every child a fair chance, and creates a classroom where no learner feels left behind.

My first philosophy statement, written during my Year One school observation, still guides me. I believe teaching is about helping every child to learn. I will use simple methods so all learners can understand, and I will encourage and support them to do their best and become confident. I also believe learning should be active and connected to real life.

Through discussions with my mentor and STS partner, I learned that teaching should be learner-centred and consider learners' needs and backgrounds. I also learned the importance of inclusion and of using different strategies so that all children can take part. Setting high expectations with support helps learners gain confidence, continuous assessment and reflection improve teaching and learning, and working with parents and the community supports learners' progress.

As an aspiring teacher, I will keep learning and improving so that I can help my learners better.
