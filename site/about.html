My Teaching Beliefs

My teaching is guided by three principles: plan with purpose, teach for understanding, and reflect to improve.

I believe learners learn best by doing and discussing. When learners take part in activities, ask questions and share ideas with one another, they understand a topic instead of only memorising it.

My goal is to build a classroom where every learner is confident, curious and successful.

After every lesson, I want to look back honestly and keep improving.
